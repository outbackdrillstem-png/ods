GST

In Australia you must register once your turnover — gross sales, not profit — hits $75,000 over a rolling 12 months, looking either back or forward. Until you register you must not charge GST, and your document is an invoice rather than a tax invoice.

Adds a 10% GST line and labels the document a tax invoice.
Leave this off if your stock prices are ex GST, which is normal for trade. GST is then added on top rather than taken out.

The date your registration takes effect, off your ATO confirmation. Anything you sold before it was not a taxable sale, so the app warns you if a document is dated earlier.

Voided invoices

Log a trip

Two ways to claim vehicle costs. Cents per kilometre needs no logbook but caps at 5,000 business kilometres a year. The logbook method needs 12 straight weeks of records and then lets you claim that business percentage of your actual running costs, which usually comes out well ahead on a working ute.

Record an auction sale

When an agent or auction house sells your gear, they issue the tax invoice, not you. Work off their settlement statement or RCTI. The figure that goes on your BAS is the gross the gear made, not the net that hit your bank — their commission and fees are a separate purchase you claim GST back on.

Deductions off the statement

Commission, yard or lot fees, insurance, advertising, cartage, levies — every line they took out. Untick GST on anything that is a government levy or otherwise GST-free.
